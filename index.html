About Stephanie

Sixteen years of social work shaped how I sell homes.

Before real estate, I spent sixteen years as a social worker with Children Services. I learned how to listen, how to stay calm in heavy moments, and how to advocate without losing my warmth. That training shapes every transaction I'm part of.

I specialize in serving families through life transitions and seniors who are downsizing. As a certified Seniors Real Estate Specialist (SRES®), I understand the financial, emotional, and logistical layers that make a move feel overwhelming. My job is to make those layers feel manageable.

If you want a realtor who will push you into a quick sale, I'm not the right fit. If you want a steady, ethical partner who treats your timeline with respect, let's talk.

Ethics

Honest pricing, honest advice, honest timelines.

Patience

Major moves shouldn't feel rushed. I'll wait with you.

Empathy

I sold my own family home through grief. I get it.

How we'll work together

A calm, predictable process.

01

Conversation

A no-obligation call or coffee. We talk about what you need, your timeline, and any concerns you're holding. I won't ask you to sign anything yet.

02

Walk-through

For sellers: I tour your home and give you a candid view of pricing, prep, and timing. For buyers: we tour properties together and refine what matters.

03

Strategy

A clear written plan with the listing price (or buying budget), the photography and marketing schedule, and what to expect each week.

04

Closing

I'm at every showing, every inspection, every signing. You'll never feel like you're navigating this alone.
